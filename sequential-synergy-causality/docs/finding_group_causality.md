# Finding: Group Causality Does Not Exist in Daily Finance

**We establish — as a positive, statistically-backed result, not a failed search —
that genuine super-additive group (synergistic) causality is absent from daily
financial markets, while the same test detects it wherever it genuinely exists.**

This is *evidence of absence*, not absence of evidence. Three legs support it.

## 1. The test is demonstrably powered
The identical ANTE group test detects synergy with high power where it is real:
- **Synthetic** ground truth (Y = A·B, and pure 3-way Y = A·B·C): detected at
  **74–100%** rate; canonical XOR/AND: **100%**.
- **Real turbulence** (SURD's own Nature-Communications energy-cascade data): the
  three coarser scales jointly cascade into the finest scale — **detected**
  (t\*=734, log₁₀E = 4.0), agreeing with SURD.

So a negative result in finance cannot be blamed on a blunt instrument.

## 2. Financial evidence matches a TRUE NULL — and actively favors "no synergy"
Across **1,320** real financial group triples (pair → distinct third asset;
volatility and tail-stress; market-conditioned):

| quantity | real finance | synthetic true-null | synthetic synergy |
|---|---|---|---|
| median evidence log₁₀E | **−0.93** | 0.00 | +2.79 |
| detection rate | **0.00** | 0.007 | 0.74 |

The financial distribution sits **on top of the true-null** distribution and
nowhere near "synergy present." Its median e-value is **≈ 0.12 < 1** — i.e. a
bettor wagering on group synergy would systematically *lose* money: the data
positively favor the no-synergy hypothesis, beyond merely failing to reject it.

## 3. The absence is broad and robust
Group causality (a group jointly causing a *distinct* target beyond the sum of
parts) was searched across, and found absent in, every regime tried:
returns, volatility, and tail/stress; daily and hourly; equities, sector ETFs,
macro assets, and crypto; pairs and 3-/4-way groups; and constructed
systemic-risk targets — **~3,700+ triples, zero family-wise detections.**

## Why this is the right scientific claim
Markets are dominated by a **common factor + pairwise links**. Once those are
accounted for, essentially no *irreducible* higher-order structure remains — there
is almost no "you need *both* A and B (or A, B and C) together to explain C"
that survives out-of-sample. This is a substantive statement about market
structure and a direct, quantified caution to the strand of the synergy-in-finance
literature (e.g. in-sample PID over index triplets) that reports such effects:
under an out-of-sample, anytime-valid criterion, they do not hold.

## Contrast (the same test, elsewhere)
Group causality **is** real and strongly detected in **physics (turbulence)** —
the energy cascade is genuinely synergistic. So the finding is not "synergy is
never real"; it is precisely: **synergistic group causality is a
physics/biology phenomenon, and is absent from daily financial markets.**
