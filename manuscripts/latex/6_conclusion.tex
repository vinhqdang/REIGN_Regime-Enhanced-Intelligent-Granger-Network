\section{Conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced the Regime-Enhanced Intelligent Granger Network (REIGN), a highly robust framework for discovering causal architectures in nonstationary time series. By hybridizing statistical changepoint detection with an augmented-Lagrangian GNN engine, REIGN seamlessly adapts to structural market breaks. Furthermore, its unique capacity to ingest structural priors from Large Language Models provides a critical bridge between human domain knowledge and deep learning. Our empirical evaluations confirm that REIGN significantly outperforms classical state-of-the-art methodologies such as PCMCI+. Moving forward, REIGN provides a reliable, interpretable foundation for deploying causal AI in high-stakes financial environments.
