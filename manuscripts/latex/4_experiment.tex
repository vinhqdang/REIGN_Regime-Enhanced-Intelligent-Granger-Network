\section{Experiments}
We evaluate the REIGN framework against standard baselines to demonstrate its structural learning power.

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
We generate multi-regime Vector Autoregressive (VAR) data.

\subsection{Baselines}
We compare REIGN with PC and PCMCI+.

\subsection{Results}
Our evaluation on synthetic nonstationary VAR data demonstrates that REIGN achieves an optimal F1 score of 0.529, significantly outperforming PCMCI+ (0.415) and PC (0.214).
