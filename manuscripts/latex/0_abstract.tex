\begin{abstract}
Causal discovery in nonstationary multivariate financial time series is challenging due to the dynamic nature of financial markets and the occurrence of structural breaks. Traditional algorithms often fail to adapt to rapidly changing regimes. We propose the Regime-Enhanced Intelligent Granger Network (REIGN), a novel framework that integrates statistical changepoint detection with Large Language Model (LLM) structural priors and a Coarse-to-Fine Neural Granger engine. Empirical evaluation on synthetic nonstationary Vector Autoregressive (VAR) data demonstrates that REIGN significantly outperforms state-of-the-art baselines across multiple structural metrics, establishing a new benchmark for time-series causal discovery.
\end{abstract}
