\section{Discussion}

The empirical evaluation clearly validates the necessity of regime-aware neural causal architectures. In this section, we analyze the specific architectural decisions via an ablation study and address the computational tradeoffs of the REIGN framework.

\subsection{Ablation Study}

To understand the contribution of individual framework components, we conducted a targeted ablation study focusing on the neural engine's topological flexibility.

As illustrated in Table \ref{tab:ablation}, removing the PELT-driven regime ensemble forces the network to learn a single compromised mapping across conflicting market distributions, immediately dropping the optimal F1 score to 0.440. More notably, reinstating the classical LASSO coarse-discovery bottleneck (a standard prerequisite in previous works like CUTS+) artificially truncated valid continuous signals before they reached the GNN, suppressing the F1 score to 0.485. By feeding the fully connected graph directly to the Message-Passing layers, the full REIGN model maximized the neural network's expressive capacity.

\subsection{Computational Complexity}
While REIGN achieves superior structural recovery, it inherently carries higher computational complexity than classical constraint-based methods. The core bottleneck lies in the Augmented Lagrangian optimization of the GNN, which requires repeated inversions or trace calculations over the $N \times N$ adjacency matrix, yielding an $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ computational cost per regime. For large-scale macro-economic datasets where $N > 100$, calculating the matrix exponential $e^{\mathbf{W} \circ \mathbf{W}}$ becomes prohibitively expensive.

\subsection{The Role of Zero-Shot LLM Priors}
It is essential to highlight that the state-of-the-art results demonstrated in Section IV were achieved in an environment where the LLM prior penalty ($\lambda_{prior}$) was effectively neutralized to isolate the standalone power of the neural engine. In real-world deployments, nonstationary data is often so sparse that purely data-driven discovery is mathematically underdetermined. The true industry potential of REIGN lies in its capability to request zero-shot topological skeletons from foundational models like Anthropic Claude. By injecting these priors into the continuous loss landscape, researchers can anchor the GNN against statistical noise, significantly boosting sample efficiency.
